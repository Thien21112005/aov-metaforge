## Scope

Whole app shell plus gacha, draft, compendium, hero detail. Visitor mode: Operate. Replaces the previous light glass look.

## Audience, job, constraints

Rank players on phones rolling comps; coaches on desktop running a draft with the analysis in view. Static vanilla JS, no build. VI/EN UI toggle; hero data stays Vietnamese. Game-feel additions approved by the owner: turn timer, draft persistence, screenshot-ready result.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The app is forged from one rank medal: lapis enamel, thin gilt, and a heated seam that traces the 1-2-2-2-2-1 snake. It refuses both the light SaaS card stack and a copy of Garena's own lobby.

OWN-WORLD: Midnight lapis ground, team fields of enamel blue and oxblood, gilt hairlines at 3px corners, gold reserved for current turn and S+, ember orange only on the active seam leg. Saira Condensed for names and numerals, Be Vietnam Pro for reading. Faceted tier medals. Drawn lane icons, no emoji.

STORY: A player sees whose turn it is, picks from the grid without losing the board, and reads a one-line verdict per lane. A gacha player watches staggered reels land on large portraits and screenshots the team.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top nav with language, music, sound at right. Draft: ban rows left and right of a central clock and turn line; blue rail left, red rail right with five portrait slots; centre column holds the seam, search and lane filters, hero grid, verdict strip. Gacha: mode control and gold roll action on one row, then reels filling the height.

FORM: Candidate 4 of 7, enamel rank medal. seed:89860a5fd5fb277d. Raises from orienteering map (one colour reserved for the active leg), ticket wallet (cancelled things stay visible, struck), doujin catalog (dense whole-cell thumbnail grid).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
